# Trendline Trading in Crypto: The Visual Edge

Trendlines are the simplest and most effective tool in technical analysis. Two points create a line. That line tells you the trend, provides entry points, and signals when the trend is ending.

## How to Draw Valid Trendlines

### Rules for Valid Trendlines
1. **Minimum 2 touches** (3+ is better)
2. **Connect swing lows** (uptrend) or **swing highs** (downtrend)
3. **Use candle bodies or wicks** — be consistent
4. **Don't force it** — if the line doesn't fit naturally, it's not a trendline

### Uptrend Trendline
Connect at least two ascending swing lows. Each low should be higher than the previous.

### Downtrend Trendline
Connect at least two descending swing highs. Each high should be lower than the previous.

### Channel
Parallel lines connecting both highs and lows. Price bounces between them.

## Trendline Trading Strategies

### Strategy 1: Trendline Bounce
The highest probability trendline trade:
```
Uptrend: Price pulls back to ascending trendline → Buy
Entry: Bullish candle at trendline
Stop: Below trendline (1-2%)
Target: Previous high or channel resistance
```

### Strategy 2: Trendline Break
When price breaks through a trendline, the trend may be changing:
```
Price breaks below ascending trendline with volume
Wait for retest of broken trendline (now resistance)
Enter short on rejection at trendline
Target: Next support level
```

### Strategy 3: Channel Trading
Trade within parallel channels:
```
Buy at channel support (lower trendline)
Sell at channel resistance (upper trendline)
Stop: Outside the channel
Works until the channel breaks
```

## Common Trendline Mistakes

1. **Too many trendlines**: 2-3 per chart maximum
2. **Forcing trendlines**: If you need more than 3 points to justify it, it's not real
3. **Ignoring trendline breaks**: When a valid trendline breaks with volume, respect it
4. **Using very short timeframes**: Trendlines on 1-minute charts are mostly noise. Daily+ is best.

## FAQ

### How many touches make a trendline valid?
Two touches create the line; three or more validate it. Each additional touch increases reliability. On the daily chart, a trendline with 4+ touches over several weeks is very strong.

### When does a trendline break confirm a trend change?
A close (not wick) beyond the trendline with above-average [volume](/blog/crypto-volume-analysis-guide). Wait for a retest of the broken trendline for confirmation. A failed retest confirms the break is real.

# Crypto Portfolio Rebalancing Strategies

Your portfolio drifts from its target allocation every day as prices move. A 60/40 BTC/ETH split can become 80/20 after a BTC rally. Rebalancing restores your intended risk profile and systematically sells high, buys low.

## Why Rebalance?

Without rebalancing, winners grow to dominate your portfolio. This feels great in a bull market but concentrates risk. When BTC is 90% of your portfolio and it crashes 40%, your entire portfolio takes a 36% hit.

### Rebalancing Benefits
- **Systematic profit-taking**: Automatically trims winners
- **Buy-the-dip built-in**: Automatically adds to underperformers
- **Risk control**: Maintains your target risk level
- **Removes emotion**: Rules-based, not feeling-based

## Rebalancing Strategies

### 1. Calendar Rebalancing
Rebalance on a fixed schedule:
```
Monthly: Check allocations on the 1st
Quarterly: Rebalance every 3 months
Annually: Once per year (lowest effort)
```
Best for: Passive investors who don't watch markets daily.

### 2. Threshold Rebalancing
Rebalance when any asset drifts beyond a threshold:
```
Target: 50% BTC / 30% ETH / 20% Stablecoins
Threshold: ±5%
Trigger: BTC hits 55% or drops to 45% → Rebalance
```
Best for: Active investors who want tighter risk control.

### 3. Tactical Rebalancing
Adjust targets based on market conditions:
```
Bull market (Fear & Greed > 70): Shift to 40/20/40 (more stables)
Normal market: Maintain 50/30/20
Bear market (Fear & Greed < 30): Shift to 60/30/10 (more crypto)
```
See [sentiment analysis guide](/blog/crypto-market-sentiment-analysis-guide).

## Practical Example

Starting portfolio: $10,000
- BTC: $5,000 (50%)
- ETH: $3,000 (30%)
- USDC: $2,000 (20%)

After BTC rallies 40%, ETH flat:
- BTC: $7,000 (58%)
- ETH: $3,000 (25%)
- USDC: $2,000 (17%)
- Total: $12,000

Rebalance to targets:
- Sell $1,000 BTC → Buy $600 ETH + $400 USDC
- New: BTC $6,000 (50%) / ETH $3,600 (30%) / USDC $2,400 (20%)

You just locked in $1,000 of BTC profits without trying to time the top.

## FAQ

### How often should I rebalance my crypto portfolio?
Monthly or quarterly works well for most investors. More frequent rebalancing (weekly) increases transaction costs without significantly improving returns. Threshold-based rebalancing (±5%) is often the best compromise.

### Does rebalancing reduce returns?
In a sustained bull market, yes — you're selling winners. But over full cycles (bull + bear), rebalancing typically improves risk-adjusted returns because it prevents catastrophic concentration losses during crashes.

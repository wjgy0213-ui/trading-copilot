# Fear & Greed Index Below 10: Ultimate Guide to Trading in Panic Capitulation

When the Crypto Fear & Greed Index crashes below 10, you're witnessing **panic capitulation**—the moment when retail investors surrender and sell at any price. History shows this is when fortunes are made. But one wrong move, and you'll catch a falling knife. This guide teaches you how to navigate single-digit fear levels like a professional.

## Understanding Fear & Greed Below 10

### What Fear < 10 Really Means

The Fear & Greed Index ranges from 0-100:
- **75-100**: Extreme Greed
- **55-74**: Greed
- **45-54**: Neutral
- **25-44**: Fear
- **10-24**: Extreme Fear
- **0-9**: **Panic Capitulation** 🚨

**When Fear drops below 10:**
- Retail panic selling accelerates
- Margin liquidations cascade
- Social media turns apocalyptic
- "Bitcoin is dead" headlines everywhere
- Even bulls start questioning their thesis

**But here's the paradox**: Fear < 10 has been the **best buying opportunity** in crypto history.

## Trading Strategies for Fear < 10

### Strategy 1: Capitulation Volume Entry

**Concept**: Buy when panic volume peaks, then drops.

**Setup**:
1. Wait for Fear < 10 for 2+ days
2. Watch for **volume spike** (2-3x normal)
3. Enter when volume **drops 40%+** from peak
4. This signals exhaustion

**Example (FTX Collapse, Nov 2022)**:
```
Day 1: Fear = 6, Volume 100M → Wait
Day 2: Fear = 8, Volume 180M → Peak panic
Day 3: Fear = 10, Volume 80M → Entry! (Volume dropped 55%)
Result: BTC $15,500 → $18,500 in 10 days (+19%)
```

**Risk management**:
- Position size: 10-20% of capital
- Stop-loss: 12% below entry
- Take profit: 30% gain (or trail)

### Strategy 2: Dollar-Cost Averaging into Fear

**Concept**: Deploy capital in tranches as Fear deepens.

**Allocation**:
```
Fear 15-10: 30% of capital
Fear 10-5:  50% of capital
Fear < 5:   20% of capital (rare, aggressive)
```

**Example**:
```
Capital: $10,000 allocated for "Fear Buying"

Week 1: Fear = 14 → Buy $0 (wait for <10)
Week 2: Fear = 10 → Buy $3,000 (30%)
Week 3: Fear = 8  → Buy $5,000 (50%)
Week 4: Fear = 7  → Buy $2,000 (20%)

Total invested: $10,000
Entries: $69,500 avg (vs. one lump sum at $70K+)
```

**Advantages**:
- Averages into panic
- Reduces timing risk
- Emotionally easier (no FOMO)

### Strategy 3: Mean Reversion Swing Trade

**Entry rules**:
1. Fear < 10 for 3+ days
2. RSI(14) < 25 on daily
3. Price 20%+ below 50-day MA
4. Volume spike followed by decline

**Exit rules**:
- Target 1: Fear rises to 20-25 (take 50%)
- Target 2: Fear rises to 35-40 (take 30%)
- Target 3: RSI > 65 (exit final 20%)
- Stop-loss: 10% below entry

**Typical hold time**: 2-8 weeks

**Risk/Reward**: 1:3 (risk 10%, aim for 30%+)

### Strategy 4: Long-Term Accumulation

**For investors, not traders**:

**Premise**: Fear < 10 = generational buying opportunity.

**Action**:
- Deploy 50-100% of long-term capital
- Hold for 12-24+ months
- Ignore daily volatility
- DCA weekly until Fear > 30

**Historical results**:
- Mar 2020 buyer: +800% in 12 months
- Nov 2022 buyer: +300% in 15 months
- May 2021 buyer: +120% in 9 months

**Key**: You must **survive** the drawdown and hold.

## Avoiding the Knife-Catching Trap

### Risks of Buying Fear < 10

Just because Fear is at 10 doesn't mean it can't go lower:

**What can go wrong**:
- ❌ Fear drops to 5 (more pain ahead)
- ❌ Price breaks critical support (freefall)
- ❌ Systemic crisis (exchange hacks, bans)
- ❌ Bear market lasts 12+ months (2018-2019)

**How to avoid catching knives**:

**1. Wait for volume confirmation**
- Don't buy on first Fear < 10 day
- Wait for capitulation volume spike
- Then wait for volume to **drop** (exhaustion)

**2. Use stop-losses**
- Even at Fear = 10, use stops (8-12% below)
- Markets can overshoot in panic
- Protect capital for second entry

**3. Watch critical support levels**
- BTC: $65K, $60K, $55K (Q1 2026)
- If support breaks, wait for new level to hold

**4. Monitor systemic risks**
- Exchange solvency (another FTX?)
- Regulatory bans (China-style crackdowns?)
- Stablecoin depegs (USDT/USDC issues?)

**5. Scale in, don't lump sum**
- Use tranches (30%/50%/20%)
- Preserve capital for "Fear = 5" scenario

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Buying on First Fear < 10 Day

❌ **Wrong**: "Fear hit 10, I'm all-in!"
✅ **Right**: "Fear hit 10, I'll wait for volume confirmation."

**Why**: Fear can drop from 10 → 5. Wait for exhaustion.

### Mistake 2: No Stop-Loss

❌ **Wrong**: "This is the bottom, no need for stops."
✅ **Right**: "I'll use a 10% stop, just in case."

**Why**: Markets can overshoot. Protect capital.

### Mistake 3: Using Leverage

❌ **Wrong**: "Fear = 10, I'll use 10x leverage for max gains!"
✅ **Right**: "Spot only. Too much volatility for leverage."

**Why**: Fear < 10 means liquidations everywhere. Don't be one of them.

### Mistake 4: Selling Too Early

❌ **Wrong**: "Fear rose to 15, I'm taking profit."
✅ **Right**: "Fear needs to hit 20-25 before I consider selling."

**Why**: Best gains come when Fear recovers to 35-50.

### Mistake 5: Ignoring Risk Management

❌ **Wrong**: "This is a once-in-a-decade opportunity, I'm using 100% of my net worth!"
✅ **Right**: "I'll risk 20-30% of capital, keep rest for emergencies."

**Why**: Even Fear < 10 can be a bull trap in prolonged bear markets.

# Trading Crypto News Events: The Event-Driven Playbook

News moves crypto markets faster and more violently than almost any other asset class. A single tweet can move BTC 5%. An FOMC decision can trigger a 10% swing in hours.

## The Crypto News Calendar

### High-Impact Events (Expect 3-10% moves)
| Event | Frequency | Why It Matters |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| FOMC Rate Decision | 8x/year | Interest rates affect all risk assets |
| CPI Inflation Data | Monthly | Guides Fed policy expectations |
| Bitcoin ETF Flow Data | Daily | Direct buying/selling pressure |
| Major Hack/Exploit | Random | Immediate panic selling |
| Regulatory Action | Random | Can reshape entire sectors |

### Medium-Impact Events
| Event | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Jobs Report (NFP) | Macro sentiment shift |
| Token Unlocks | Supply increase → selling pressure |
| Exchange Listings | Short-term pump on listed token |
| Protocol Upgrades | Narrative-driven moves |
| Earnings (Coinbase, MicroStrategy) | Proxy for crypto health |

## News Trading Strategies

### Strategy 1: Pre-Positioning
Take a position based on expected outcome:
```
CPI expected lower → Risk-on → Pre-position long BTC
Stop: 2% below entry (in case CPI is hot)
Target: 3-5% move on favorable data
```
⚠️ Risk: If the outcome is opposite to expectations, losses are immediate and sharp.

### Strategy 2: Straddle (Volatility Play)
Profit from the move regardless of direction:
```
Before FOMC: Buy calls AND puts on IBIT options
Cost: Combined premium
Profit if: BTC moves > combined premium in either direction
Best when: Market expects a boring outcome but surprise is possible
```
See our [options guide](/blog/crypto-options-trading-beginners).

### Strategy 3: Fade the Spike
Wait for the initial overreaction, then trade the reversal:
```
CPI comes in hot → BTC dumps 4% in 5 minutes
Wait 15-30 minutes for dust to settle
If BTC finds support → Enter long for mean reversion
Target: Recover 50-75% of the initial dump
```

### Strategy 4: Buy the Rumor, Sell the News
The most reliable news pattern in crypto:
```
Catalyst approaching (ETF approval, upgrade, halving)
Price rallies into the event (anticipation buying)
Event occurs → Price drops (profit-taking)
Action: Sell 50% of position day before event
```

## News Trading Rules

1. **Reduce size**: Use 50% of normal position size during news events
2. **Widen stops**: Volatility expands — normal stops will get hit by noise
3. **Don't chase**: If you missed the initial move, wait for the next setup
4. **Know the calendar**: Check [Trading Copilot's market health](/health) for upcoming events
5. **Have a plan for both outcomes**: Know what you'll do if data is bullish OR bearish

## FAQ

### Should I trade during news events or avoid them?
For beginners: avoid them entirely. For experienced traders: trade with reduced size and wider stops. The opportunity is real but so is the risk. News events create the widest spreads, fastest moves, and most slippage of any market condition.

### How quickly does crypto react to news?
Seconds. Algorithmic traders react within milliseconds on major exchanges. By the time you read the headline, the initial move has happened. Focus on the second reaction (reversal or continuation) rather than trying to beat the algos.

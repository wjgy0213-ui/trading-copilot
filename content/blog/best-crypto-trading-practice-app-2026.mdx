## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is paper trading really useful?

Yes. Research consistently shows that traders who practice before going live have significantly better outcomes. The key is treating paper trading seriously — with realistic position sizes, proper risk management, and journaling every trade.

### How long should I paper trade before going live?

Most trading educators recommend **3-6 months** of consistent paper trading. With AI coaching tools like Trading Copilot, this timeline can be shortened to 2-4 months because you get instant feedback and structured progression.

### Can I learn to trade crypto without losing money?

You can learn the **mechanics** without losing money through paper trading. However, managing real emotions (fear, greed, FOMO) requires small real-money positions eventually. The ideal path: paper trade → micro positions ($10-50) → gradually increase.

### What's the difference between paper trading and backtesting?

**Paper trading** is forward-looking: you make decisions in real-time with live market data. **Backtesting** is backward-looking: you test a strategy against historical data. Both are valuable — backtest first, then paper trade to validate.

# Crypto Scalping: High-Frequency Strategy Guide

Scalping is the fastest form of active trading — holding positions for seconds to minutes, targeting tiny gains that compound through volume. It's not for everyone, but done right, it's one of the most consistent strategies in crypto.

## What Is Scalping?

- **Hold time**: Seconds to 15 minutes
- **Target per trade**: 0.1-0.5% (10-50 pips)
- **Trades per day**: 10-50+
- **Timeframes**: 1-minute, 5-minute, tick charts
- **Win rate goal**: 55-65%
- **R:R**: 1:1 to 1.5:1

## Why Scalping Works in Crypto

1. **24/7 markets**: Scalp any time, any session
2. **High volatility**: More movement = more scalping opportunities
3. **Deep liquidity** (on major pairs): BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT fill instantly
4. **Low maker fees**: 0.01-0.02% on competitive exchanges
5. **No overnight risk**: All positions closed by end of session

## Scalping Requirements

### Capital
- **Minimum**: $5,000-$10,000 (with 5-10x leverage)
- **Comfortable**: $25,000+ (lower leverage needed)
- **Per-trade profit**: $5-50 per scalp at proper sizing

### Exchange
Choose based on:
- **Low fees**: Hyperliquid (0.01% maker) or Binance with BNB discount
- **Fast execution**: Order-to-fill < 50ms
- **Deep order books**: BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT
- **Reliable**: No downtime during volatile moments

### Tools
- Level 2 order book (real-time depth)
- Time & Sales (tape reader)
- Multiple timeframe charts (1m + 5m + 15m)
- Hotkey execution (keyboard order placement)
- Low-latency internet connection

### Psychology
- **Focus**: 2-4 hour sessions max (concentration degrades)
- **Discipline**: Take stops immediately, no "hoping"
- **Detachment**: Each trade is meaningless; the series matters
- **Rules-based**: Zero discretionary exceptions during session

## Core Scalping Strategies

### 1. Support/Resistance Bounce
Trade bounces off key levels on the 1-minute chart:
```
Setup: Price approaches 1m support with volume declining
Entry: First bullish candle bouncing off support
Stop: Below support (tight, 0.15-0.25%)
Target: Previous minor resistance (0.2-0.4%)
```

### 2. Breakout Scalp
Trade initial momentum after level break:
```
Setup: 5m consolidation near resistance
Entry: First 1m candle closing above resistance with volume
Stop: Below breakout candle (0.1-0.2%)
Target: Measured move equal to consolidation range
Exit: If momentum stalls within 3 candles
```

### 3. Mean Reversion
Fade overextended moves back to VWAP or moving average:
```
Setup: Price >2 ATR from VWAP on 1m
Entry: Reversal candle pattern (engulfing, pin bar)
Stop: Beyond the extreme (0.2-0.3%)
Target: VWAP (0.3-0.6%)
```

### 4. Order Flow Scalp
Read the tape (Level 2 / Time & Sales):
```
Setup: Large resting orders at a price level (visible wall)
Entry: When aggressive buying hits the wall and price doesn't break
Stop: If wall gets pulled (0.1%)
Target: Opposite wall (0.2-0.4%)
```

## Scalping Risk Management

| Rule | Parameter | Why |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Max risk/trade | 0.5-1% of account | Small losses, fast recovery |
| Max daily loss | 2-3% | Prevents tilt destruction |
| Max consecutive losses | 3 → take 15 min break | Cool down period |
| Max trades/day | 30-50 | Overtrading prevention |
| Position sizing | Fixed size per trade | No sizing based on emotion |

**The 3-strike rule**: Three consecutive losses = mandatory 15-minute break. Walk away from the screen. This single rule prevents most scalping blowups.

## Scalping Mistakes

### 1. Ignoring Fees
If your average gain is 0.1% and fees are 0.1% round-trip, you're trading for free. Use maker orders and low-fee exchanges.

### 2. Widening Stops
A scalp stop should never be moved. If it's hit, take the loss. The next opportunity is seconds away.

### 3. Turning Scalps into Swing Trades
"Let me hold a bit longer" turns a -0.2% scalp loss into a -5% swing loss. Close positions within your timeframe.

### 4. Trading Low-Liquidity Pairs
Scalping SHIB/USDT with wide spreads eats your edge. Stick to BTC and ETH until you're consistently profitable.

### 5. No Daily Loss Limit
Without a hard stop, one bad session can erase a week of gains. Set it, honor it, always.

## FAQ

### Is scalping profitable in crypto?
Yes, for disciplined traders with low-fee access. The math: 30 trades/day × 55% win rate × 1.2:1 R:R = approximately 0.5-1% daily account growth after fees. That compounds significantly. However, most people lack the discipline and focus required — practice extensively before risking real capital.

### How much money do I need to start scalping crypto?
$5,000 minimum with 5-10x leverage on BTC/ETH. With less, your per-trade profits are too small to be meaningful after fees. With more ($25K+), you can use lower leverage and take more comfortable position sizes.

### What timeframe is best for scalping?
1-minute charts for entries and exits, 5-minute for context, 15-minute for trend direction. Some scalpers also use tick charts or time & sales for execution timing. Don't use anything longer than 15-minute — that's [swing trading](/blog/crypto-swing-trading-strategy-guide) territory.

### Can you scalp crypto with a bot?
Yes, algorithmic scalping eliminates the psychological component but introduces technical risk (bugs, API failures, exchange outages). Start with manual scalping to understand the patterns, then automate proven strategies. See our [automation guide](/blog/crypto-trading-automation-guide).

# Market Structure: Read the Trend Like a Pro

Market structure is the backbone of price action trading. Before you look at any indicator, understand the structure: Is price making higher highs and higher lows (uptrend) or lower highs and lower lows (downtrend)?

## The Basics

### Uptrend Structure
- **Higher Highs (HH)**: Each peak is higher than the last
- **Higher Lows (HL)**: Each pullback bottom is higher than the last
- As long as HH + HL continue, the uptrend is intact

### Downtrend Structure
- **Lower Highs (LH)**: Each peak is lower than the last
- **Lower Lows (LL)**: Each bottom is lower than the last
- As long as LH + LL continue, the downtrend is intact

### Range/Consolidation
- Equal highs and equal lows
- No clear directional structure
- Wait for a break before trading directionally

## Key Concepts

### Break of Structure (BOS)
Price breaks beyond the last significant high (uptrend) or low (downtrend), **continuing** the trend.
```
Uptrend BOS: Price breaks above the last HH → Trend continues → Look for buy on pullback
Downtrend BOS: Price breaks below the last LL → Trend continues → Look for sell on pullback
```

### Change of Character (CHoCH)
Price breaks structure in the **opposite** direction, signaling potential trend change.
```
Uptrend CHoCH: Price breaks below the last HL → Trend may be reversing to downtrend
Downtrend CHoCH: Price breaks above the last LH → Trend may be reversing to uptrend
```

### Order Blocks
The last [candlestick](/blog/crypto-candlestick-patterns-guide) before a strong move. These areas act as supply (resistance) or demand (support) zones where institutional orders were placed.

## Trading Market Structure

### Strategy: Structure + Pullback
```
1. Identify trend (HH/HL or LH/LL)
2. Wait for pullback to previous structure level
3. Enter in trend direction on confirmation
4. Stop below structure (below HL in uptrend)
5. Target: Next structure level
```

### Entry Rules
- **Only trade WITH structure** (buy in uptrends, sell in downtrends)
- **Wait for CHoCH** before switching direction
- **Pullbacks to previous BOS levels** are highest-probability entries
- Combine with [Fibonacci](/blog/crypto-fibonacci-trading-guide) for precision

## Multi-Timeframe Structure

| Timeframe | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|

**Rule**: Trade in the direction of the daily structure. Use 4H/1H for entries. See [timeframes guide](/blog/crypto-trading-timeframes-guide).

## FAQ

### What's the difference between market structure and indicators?
Market structure is raw price action — it doesn't lag because it IS the price. Indicators like [moving averages](/blog/crypto-moving-averages-strategy) and [RSI](/blog/crypto-rsi-divergence-strategy) are derived from price and always lag. Learn structure first, then use indicators as confirmation.

### How do I identify a CHoCH in real-time?
A CHoCH occurs when price breaks below the last higher low (in an uptrend) or above the last lower high (in a downtrend). Mark these levels in advance. When price closes beyond them with [volume](/blog/crypto-volume-analysis-guide), the character has changed. Don't anticipate it — wait for confirmation.

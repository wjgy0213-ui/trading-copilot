# Stablecoin Yield Strategies: Earn 4-12% on Your Crypto

Stablecoins sitting in your wallet earn 0%. Meanwhile, you can earn 4-12% with varying levels of risk. The key is understanding exactly what risk you're taking for each yield.

## The Yield Spectrum

| Strategy | APY Range | Risk Level | Complexity |
|----------|-----------|------------|------------|
| CEX Earn (Binance, Coinbase) | 3-6% | Low | Easy |
| On-chain lending (Aave, Compound) | 3-8% | Medium | Medium |
| LP stablecoin pairs | 5-15% | Medium-High | Medium |
| RWA protocols (Ondo, Maple) | 4-8% | Medium | Medium |
| Restaking / Points farming | 8-20%+ | High | High |

## Tier 1: Low Risk (3-6% APY)

### Centralized Exchange Earn Programs

The simplest option. Deposit stablecoins on an exchange and earn interest.

| Exchange | USDT APY | USDC APY | Lock Required? |
|----------|----------|----------|----------------|
| Binance Earn | 3.5-5% | 3-4.5% | Flexible or locked |
| Coinbase | 4.0% | 4.0% | None (USDC only) |
| Bybit Savings | 3-5% | 3-4% | Flexible |
| OKX Simple Earn | 3-5% | 3-5% | Flexible |

**Risks:**
- Exchange custody risk (your coins are on the exchange)
- Variable rates (can change anytime)
- Opportunity cost (funds locked during market moves)

**Best for**: Beginners, small amounts, or stablecoins you're keeping as dry powder for buying dips.

### T-Bill Backed Stablecoins

Newer stablecoins backed by US Treasury bills, passing yield to holders:
- **USDY** (Ondo Finance): 4-5% APY
- **sDAI** (Spark Protocol): 4-5% APY
- **USDM** (Mountain Protocol): 5% APY

**Risks**: Smart contract risk, regulatory risk, but underlying asset (T-bills) is very safe.

## Tier 2: Medium Risk (5-8% APY)

### On-Chain Lending

Deposit stablecoins into lending protocols. Borrowers pay interest, you earn yield.

| Protocol | Chain | APY Range | TVL |
|----------|-------|-----------|-----|
| Aave V3 | Multi-chain | 3-7% | $10B+ |
| Compound V3 | Ethereum | 3-6% | $3B+ |
| Morpho | Ethereum | 4-8% | $2B+ |

**Risks:**
- Smart contract risk (protocol hack)
- Utilization risk (high withdrawal demand → frozen funds)
- Oracle risk (price feed manipulation)

**Mitigation**: Stick to blue-chip protocols (Aave, Compound) with proven track records and insurance (Nexus Mutual).

### RWA (Real World Asset) Protocols

Protocols that tokenize real-world debt and pass yield to depositors:

| Protocol | Yield Source | APY |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| Ondo Finance | US Treasuries | 4-5% |
| Maple Finance | Corporate lending | 6-8% |
| Centrifuge | Real estate/invoices | 5-9% |

**Risks**: Default risk on underlying loans, smart contract risk, regulatory risk.

## Tier 3: Higher Risk (8-15%+ APY)

### Stablecoin LP Pairs

Providing liquidity to decentralized exchanges for stablecoin pairs (USDC/USDT, DAI/USDC):

**Why it pays more**: You earn trading fees + protocol incentives.

**Impermanent loss**: Minimal for stablecoin pairs (they track the same price), but not zero — depegging events can cause losses.

| Platform | Pair | APY |
|----------|------|-----|
| Curve Finance | 3pool (USDT/USDC/DAI) | 3-8% |
| Uniswap V3 | USDC/USDT | 5-15% |
| Aerodrome | USDC/USDT | 8-20% |

**Risks**: Smart contract risk, depeg risk, impermanent loss during extreme events.

### Points Farming / Restaking

Depositing stablecoins into new protocols to earn future token airdrops:

**Current examples**: Ethena (sUSDe), Pendle yield trading, EigenLayer restaking

**APY**: Highly variable, 10-30%+ including expected airdrops

**Risks**: 
- Token might not launch or have low value
- Smart contract risk (newer, less audited protocols)
- Liquidity risk (funds may be locked)
- The yield IS the risk — if it seems too good, it probably is

## The Risk Framework

Before choosing a strategy, ask:

1. **What am I risking?** (Smart contract, custody, counterparty)
2. **What's the worst case?** (100% loss, temporary lock, reduced yield)
3. **Is the yield sustainable?** (Backed by real economics or just token emissions?)
4. **Can I exit quickly?** (Lockup periods, withdrawal delays)
5. **Is the protocol audited?** (Multiple audits from reputable firms?)

### Yield Warning Signs 🚩
- APY > 20% with "no risk" claims
- Yield paid in a new, unproven token
- Protocol < 6 months old with minimal audits
- TVL growing faster than revenue
- Complex mechanics you can't explain simply

## FAQ

### Is stablecoin yield safe?
It depends on the strategy. CEX earn programs and T-bill backed stablecoins are relatively safe (3-5%). On-chain lending on blue-chip protocols carries smart contract risk but has a strong track record. High-yield farming (10%+) carries meaningful risk of partial or total loss.

### Can stablecoins lose their peg?
Yes. UST collapsed to $0 in 2022. USDC briefly depegged to $0.87 during SVB's failure. Algorithmic stablecoins are highest risk; fiat-backed (USDC, USDT) and T-bill backed are safer but not guaranteed.

### What's the best stablecoin for yield farming?
USDC is the safest choice for yield — fully backed, transparent audits, widely accepted. USDT has higher yields on some platforms but less transparency. DAI is decentralized but carries smart contract risk through MakerDAO.

### How are stablecoin yields taxed?
Yield earned on stablecoins is typically taxed as ordinary income at the fair market value when received. This applies to lending interest, LP fees, and staking rewards. See our [crypto tax guide](/blog/crypto-tax-guide-traders) for details.

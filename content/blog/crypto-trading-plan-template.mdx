## FAQ

### How detailed should my trading plan be?
Detailed enough that another trader could follow it and take the same trades. If your entry rules are vague ("buy when it looks bullish"), they're not rules. Specific and measurable beats long and complicated.

### How often should I update my trading plan?
Review monthly, update quarterly. Don't change your plan based on a single losing week — that's curve-fitting to recent results. Only update after 50+ trades show a consistent pattern that warrants adjustment.

### What if market conditions change and my plan stops working?
Every plan has market conditions where it doesn't work. When conditions are unfavorable, reduce size or stop trading — don't change the plan mid-trade. Adapt during review periods, not during live trading.

### Do professional traders use trading plans?
Every professional trader and institutional trading desk operates with a trading plan (usually called a "playbook"). The difference is that pros follow their plans religiously while amateurs create plans and then ignore them under pressure.

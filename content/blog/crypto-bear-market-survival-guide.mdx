# Bear Market Survival Guide: Protect and Profit

Bear markets separate traders from gamblers. The goal isn't to make money — it's to preserve capital and position for the next bull. Every major crypto fortune was built during bear markets by people who had the capital and conviction to buy when everyone else was selling.

## Bear Market Survival Rules

### Rule 1: Capital Preservation Is Job #1
Your only goal is to enter the next bull market with as much capital as possible.
- Reduce position sizes by 50-75%
- Increase stablecoin allocation to 50-70%
- Avoid leverage entirely (or minimal: 2x max)
- See [risk management guide](/blog/crypto-risk-management-ultimate-guide)

### Rule 2: Cut Altcoins Early
Altcoins drop 80-95% in bear markets. BTC drops 70-80%.
- At first sign of bear market: Sell most altcoins
- Keep only BTC, ETH, and stablecoins
- Don't "wait for a bounce" on altcoins — every bounce is a lower high

### Rule 3: Bear Market DCA
The best returns come from buying during maximum fear:
```
Strategy: Weekly DCA into BTC when Fear & Greed < 25
Amount: Small (1-2% of portfolio per week)
Duration: Continue throughout bear market
Expected outcome: Average entry price well below bull market highs
```

### Rule 4: Earn Yield on Stablecoins
Your capital shouldn't sit idle. See [stablecoin yield strategies](/blog/crypto-stablecoin-yield-strategies):
- 4-8% on USDC/USDT through lending
- T-bill backed stablecoin yields
- Conservative DeFi strategies

### Rule 5: Learn During Bear Markets
Bear markets last 12-18 months. Use the time to:
- Improve your [technical analysis](/blog/best-crypto-trading-indicators-2026) skills
- Build and backtest [trading strategies](/blog/backtest-crypto-strategy-guide)
- Study [market structure](/blog/crypto-market-structure-guide) and [price action](/blog/crypto-candlestick-patterns-guide)
- Paper trade new approaches

## Bear Market Trading Strategies

### Short Selling (Advanced Only)
Profit from declining prices:
- Short rallies to [resistance](/blog/crypto-support-resistance-trading) in confirmed downtrend
- Use tight stops (bear market rallies can be violent)
- Never short into [extreme fear](/blog/crypto-market-sentiment-analysis-guide)

### Range Trading
Bear markets have tradeable ranges:
```
BTC ranges between $25K-$32K for 6 months
Buy near $25K support, sell near $32K resistance
Stop: Below $24K
R:R: 2-3:1 per range trade
```

### Event-Driven Trading
Bear markets have volatility events:
- CPI/FOMC announcements → short-term opportunities
- Exchange collapses → panic bottoms → buy
- Regulatory news → knee-jerk reactions → fade

## FAQ

### How do I know we're in a bear market?
BTC is below the 200-day moving average, Fear & Greed consistently below 30, BTC dominance rising (altcoins dying), and price is making lower highs and lower lows on the weekly chart. See [market structure guide](/blog/crypto-market-structure-guide).

### Should I sell everything in a bear market?
Not everything — maintain a core BTC position (20-30% of portfolio) and keep 50-70% in stablecoins earning yield. Complete liquidation risks missing the bottom and early recovery. The goal is reduced exposure, not zero exposure.

### When does the bear market end?
Historically: 12-18 months after the cycle peak. Key signals: [extreme fear](/blog/crypto-market-sentiment-analysis-guide) persisting for months, long-term holders accumulating, trading volume hitting multi-year lows, and mainstream media declaring "crypto is dead."

This isn't a small fintech experiment. This is a **government-sponsored enterprise** (GSE) — the same entity that backs trillions in U.S. mortgages — officially recognizing crypto as legitimate collateral for home purchases.

Here's everything you need to know about crypto mortgages, how they work, and whether you should use your BTC stack to buy real estate.

### Example: $500K House Purchase with BTC

**Scenario**: You want to buy a $500,000 house using Bitcoin collateral.

#### Traditional Route (Sell BTC → Pay Cash)
- Sell **$100K in BTC** for 20% down payment
- **Capital gains tax**: ~$24K (24% bracket, assuming $50K cost basis)
- Net cash for down payment: **$76K** (need to sell more BTC to cover tax)
- **Actual BTC sold**: ~$132K worth

#### Crypto Mortgage Route (Keep BTC, Use as Collateral)
- **Down payment**: $175K in BTC collateral (35% LTV for BTC)
- **Loan amount**: $325K at 8.5% interest
- **BTC remains yours** (no capital gains tax event)
- **Crypto custody**: Coinbase Prime (you retain ownership)
- **Total BTC locked**: $175K

**Key advantage**: You keep your BTC exposure. If BTC 2x in 3 years, your collateral grows while you own the house.

## The Math: When Crypto Mortgages Make Sense

### ✅ Good Idea If:
1. **You're bullish long-term on BTC** (expect >10% annual appreciation)
2. **You have 40%+ of home value in crypto** (avoid over-leveraging)
3. **Stable income** to service mortgage payments
4. **Emergency fund** to cover margin calls if BTC crashes

### ❌ Bad Idea If:
1. **BTC is 80%+ of your net worth** (concentration risk)
2. **No income diversification** (unemployed or crypto-only income)
3. **Short-term speculation** mindset (treating house as leverage trade)
4. **Can't stomach -40% BTC drawdowns** without panic

## Risk Analysis: What Could Go Wrong?

### Risk 1: Crypto Volatility = Margin Calls
**Scenario**: BTC drops -30% in a month  
- Your $175K collateral → $122.5K  
- LTV jumps from 65% → **93%** (above 90% liquidation trigger)
- **Outcome**: Forced to add more crypto or sell BTC to reduce LTV

**Mitigation**: 
- Over-collateralize (use 45-50% instead of 35% down)
- Set up auto-alerts at 75% LTV
- Keep USDC buffer for margin calls

---

### Risk 2: Regulatory Changes
**Scenario**: Government changes crypto tax treatment or GSE rules  
- Fannie Mae forced to exit crypto mortgage market
- Existing loans grandfathered, but refinancing becomes impossible

**Mitigation**: 
- Lock in fixed-rate loan (avoid ARM)
- Have exit plan (traditional refinancing option)

---

### Risk 3: Coinbase Custody Risk
**Scenario**: Coinbase faces regulatory issues or security breach  
- Your collateral is held by Coinbase Prime
- Unlike Fannie Mae (government backstop), Coinbase is a private company

**Mitigation**: 
- Coinbase Prime has **$320M insurance** on crypto assets
- Segregated custody (your assets aren't Coinbase's liabilities)
- Consider split custody (50% Coinbase, 50% self-custody credit)

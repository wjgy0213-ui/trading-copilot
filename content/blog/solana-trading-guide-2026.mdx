## FAQ

### Is Solana a good investment in 2026?
Solana has strong fundamentals: high throughput, low fees, growing ecosystem, and potential ETF approval. However, it's still highly correlated with BTC and more volatile. Consider SOL as part of a diversified crypto portfolio, not as your only holding.

### How do I buy meme coins on Solana?
Install Phantom wallet → Fund with SOL → Go to Jupiter.ag → Paste the token's contract address → Swap SOL for the meme coin. Always start with tiny amounts and verify the contract address from official sources.

### What are the risks of trading on Solana?
Network congestion during high activity (transactions can fail), rug pulls and scam tokens (especially meme coins), smart contract risks in DeFi protocols, and the general volatility of SOL itself. Always use [proper risk management](/blog/crypto-risk-reward-ratio-guide).

### How does Solana compare to Ethereum for trading?
Solana: faster transactions, lower fees, better for frequent trading and meme coins. Ethereum: deeper liquidity, more established DeFi, better security track record, more institutional adoption. Many traders use both — ETH for larger positions and blue-chip DeFi, SOL for active trading and meme coins.

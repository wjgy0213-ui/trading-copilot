# Altcoin Season: When to Rotate Capital

Altcoin season is when altcoins outperform Bitcoin by 10-50x in weeks. Catching it early can multiply your portfolio faster than holding BTC alone. Missing it means watching others get rich while your BTC grinds sideways.

## What Is Altcoin Season?

**Definition**: A period when the majority of top 100 altcoins outperform Bitcoin over 90 days.

**Typical pattern**:
1. BTC rallies first (leads the cycle)
2. BTC consolidates sideways (profit-taking)
3. Capital rotates into ETH (large-cap alt)
4. Then into mid-caps and small-caps (altcoin explosion)
5. Finally into meme coins and garbage (cycle top signal)

**Historical examples**:
- 2017 Dec - Jan 2018: Altcoins +300-1000%, BTC +50%
- 2021 April-May: DeFi + NFT mania, many alts 10-50x

## Key Indicators

### 1. Bitcoin Dominance (BTC.D)
BTC.D = Bitcoin market cap / Total crypto market cap

```
BTC.D rising (60%+) → Capital flowing into BTC (early bull)
BTC.D falling (40-50%) → Capital rotating to alts (altcoin season)
BTC.D < 40% → Late cycle, extreme alt euphoria (often near top)
```

**Actionable rule**: When BTC.D starts declining after a BTC rally, start accumulating quality alts.

### 2. Altcoin Season Index
Track how many of top 50 altcoins outperformed BTC in last 90 days:
- **< 25%**: Bitcoin season (hold BTC)
- **25-75%**: Mixed market
- **> 75%**: Altcoin season (rotate to alts)

Tool: [Altcoin Season Index](https://www.blockchaincenter.net/en/altcoin-season-index/)

### 3. ETH/BTC Ratio
```
ETH/BTC rising → Capital leaving BTC for alts (alt season starting)
ETH/BTC falling → Risk-off, capital fleeing to BTC (alt season ending)
```

ETH is the gateway between BTC and altcoins. When ETH/BTC bottoms and reverses, altcoin season often follows within weeks.

### 4. Capital Rotation Flow
Watch where volume and price action move:
```
Week 1-2: BTC pumps, alts bleed
Week 3-4: BTC sideways, ETH starts pumping
Week 5-8: Large-cap alts (SOL, ADA, AVAX) pump
Week 9-12: Mid/small-caps explode
Week 13+: Meme coins and junk 10x (cycle top warning)
```

## How to Trade Altcoin Season

### Strategy 1: Phased Rotation
Don't go all-in on alts at once. Rotate gradually:
```
BTC rally phase: 80% BTC / 20% stablecoins
BTC consolidation: 60% BTC / 30% ETH / 10% large-cap alts
ETH/BTC rising: 40% BTC / 40% alts (ETH, SOL, etc.) / 20% stables
Full alt season: 20% BTC / 60% alts / 20% stables (dry powder)
Late cycle signals: Start rotating back to BTC and stables
```

### Strategy 2: Quality over Quantity
Focus on alts with:
- Strong fundamentals (actual product, revenue, TVL)
- Active development (GitHub commits, roadmap execution)
- Community engagement (not just bot followers)
- Prior cycle survivors (avoided going to zero)

Avoid: New launches with no track record, clones, hype-only projects.

### Strategy 3: Take Profits in Phases
Altcoins can 5x in a week and give it all back in a day:
```
+50%: Take initial investment out (now risk-free)
+100%: Sell 25% (lock gains)
+200%: Sell another 25%
+500%: Sell most, keep 10-20% lottery ticket
```

## Warning Signs Altcoin Season Is Ending

- Everyone you know is buying crypto ("my Uber driver told me about...")
- Meme coins dominating trending lists
- Projects launching with no product, just hype
- Your +500% alt starts giving back gains fast
- BTC.D starts rising again
- ETH/BTC ratio tops and reverses

When you see 3+ of these, start rotating back to BTC and stablecoins.

## FAQ

### Should I sell all my Bitcoin for altcoins during altcoin season?
No. Keep a BTC core position (20-40%). Altcoin season doesn't last forever, and when it ends, capital flows back to BTC. Having BTC lets you participate in both cycles without timing perfectly. See [portfolio rebalancing guide](/blog/crypto-portfolio-rebalancing-strategies).

### How long does altcoin season typically last?
Historically, 2-6 months. The 2017 alt season peaked in January 2018 after 3 months. The 2021 alt season had two waves (Feb-May and Aug-Nov). Don't expect it to last forever — take profits into strength.

## What is the Fear & Greed Index?

### The Basics

The **Crypto Fear & Greed Index** measures market sentiment on a 0-100 scale:

| Score | Classification | Meaning |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| 0-24 | **Extreme Fear** | Investors terrified, possible oversold |
| 25-44 | **Fear** | Cautious sentiment, risk-off |
| 45-55 | **Neutral** | Balanced market, no strong emotion |
| 56-74 | **Greed** | Optimism rising, FOMO setting in |
| 75-100 | **Extreme Greed** | Euphoria, possible overbought |

**Core Premise:** When others are fearful, be greedy. When others are greedy, be fearful. (Warren Buffett)

---

## How is the Index Calculated?

The index aggregates **6 data sources** (weighted):

| Component | Weight | What It Measures |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| **Volatility** | 25% | Current vs 30-day/90-day average volatility |
| **Market Momentum/Volume** | 25% | Buying pressure vs selling pressure |
| **Social Media** | 15% | Twitter/Reddit sentiment (mentions, engagement) |
| **Surveys** | 15% | Crypto investor polls |
| **Dominance** | 10% | Bitcoin dominance (↑ = fear, ↓ = greed) |
| **Trends** | 10% | Google search volume for crypto terms |

**Updated:** Daily at 00:00 UTC

**Data Source:** [Alternative.me](https://alternative.me/crypto/fear-and-greed-index/)

### Zone 2: Fear (25-44) — Wait & Watch

**Setup:**
- Market transitioning from panic to cautious
- Not low enough for aggressive accumulation
- Not high enough for distribution

**Strategy: Selective Buying on Dips**

**Entry Rules:**
1. Wait for intra-day dips of -5-8%
2. Check RSI(4H) <35 for confirmation
3. Set limit orders at support levels
4. Position size: 20-30% of allocated capital

**Example:**
```
F&G = 35 (Fear zone)
BTC at $72,000
Support: $69,000 (recent low)

Action:
- Set limit buy at $69,500 (2% above support)
- Position: 25% of allocated capital
- Stop: $66,000 (-5%)
- Target: $78,000 (+12%)
```

**Risk Management:**
- Smaller position sizes than extreme fear
- Tighter stops (-5-8% vs -15-20%)
- Faster profit-taking (don't wait for extreme greed)

**Historical Win Rate:** 62% (moderate, not a high-conviction zone)

### Zone 3: Neutral (45-55) — Range Trading

**Setup:**
- Balanced market, no strong directional bias
- Often seen in consolidation phases

**Strategy: Range-Bound Scalping**

**Entry Rules:**
1. Identify clear support/resistance range
2. Buy at range bottom, sell at range top
3. Quick 5-10% swings

**Example:**
```
F&G = 50 (Neutral)
BTC range: $70,000 - $75,000

Action:
- Buy at $70,500 (range low)
- Sell at $74,500 (range high)
- Profit: ~5.7%
- Repeat 3-5 times per month
```

**Risk Management:**
- Stop below range support (-3-5%)
- Take profit at range resistance
- Exit if range breaks (new trend forming)

**Historical Win Rate:** 54% (low conviction, avoid if unclear range)

### Zone 4: Greed (56-74) — Riding Momentum

**Setup:**
- Bull market in progress
- FOMO starting but not extreme

**Strategy: Trend Following + Partial Profit-Taking**

**Entry Rules:**
1. Only enter on pullbacks (not breakouts)
2. Wait for RSI(Daily) to cool to 40-50
3. Ride trend with trailing stops

**Example:**
```
F&G = 65 (Greed)
BTC at $80,000, uptrend confirmed

Action:
- Buy on -5% pullback to $76,000
- Trailing stop: 10% below recent high
- Target: Open-ended (ride trend until reversal)
- Take 20% profit every +15% move
```

**Risk Management:**
- **Start taking profits** (this is NOT accumulation phase)
- Sell 20-30% of holdings per +15-20% rally
- Tighten stop losses as greed intensifies
- **Never add new capital in greed zone** (only manage existing positions)

**Historical Win Rate:** 71% (trend following works in greed phase)

**Warning:** Greed zone is **NOT** for aggressive buying — it's for **managing existing winners**.

## Advanced: Divergences & Hidden Signals

### Bullish Divergence (Fear Decreasing, Price Still Falling)

**Signal:**
- Price making lower lows
- Fear & Greed making higher lows (e.g., 12 → 18 → 22)

**Interpretation:** Smart money accumulating despite price drop. Fear exhausting.

**Example:**
```
Week 1: BTC $70K, F&G = 12
Week 2: BTC $68K, F&G = 18 (↑ fear decreasing)
Week 3: BTC $67K, F&G = 22 (↑ fear decreasing)

Action: Start accumulating (fear divergence = bullish)
```

**Historical Win Rate:** 76% (strong bullish signal)

### Bearish Divergence (Greed Decreasing, Price Still Rising)

**Signal:**
- Price making higher highs
- Fear & Greed making lower highs (e.g., 82 → 76 → 71)

**Interpretation:** Rally losing steam, distribution happening.

**Example:**
```
Week 1: BTC $85K, F&G = 82
Week 2: BTC $88K, F&G = 76 (↓ greed cooling)
Week 3: BTC $90K, F&G = 71 (↓ greed cooling)

Action: Take profits (greed divergence = bearish)
```

**Historical Win Rate:** 69% (moderate bearish signal)

## Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

### Mistake #1: Buying Too Early in Fear Zone
**Problem:** F&G = 30 feels scary, you buy, then it drops to 10.  
**Solution:** Wait for <20 (extreme fear). Don't rush fear zone.

### Mistake #2: Not Selling in Extreme Greed
**Problem:** F&G = 85, you think "it can go higher," then crash.  
**Solution:** **Always sell 50%+ at >80.** Top calling isn't the goal — risk reduction is.

### Mistake #3: Ignoring Macro Context
**Problem:** F&G = 12, but major hack/regulation just happened → further drop.  
**Solution:** Check **why** fear exists. If fundamental (not just panic), wait.

### Mistake #4: Trading Every Move
**Problem:** Overtrading neutral zone (45-55).  
**Solution:** Only trade extremes (<25 or >70). Ignore 25-70.

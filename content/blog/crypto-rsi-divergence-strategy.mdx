# RSI Divergence: The Most Reliable Reversal Signal

RSI divergence is the single most powerful signal for catching reversals. When price makes a new extreme but RSI doesn't confirm it, something is changing beneath the surface.

## What Is RSI Divergence?

Divergence occurs when price and RSI move in opposite directions:
- **Price makes new high** but **RSI makes lower high** → Bearish divergence
- **Price makes new low** but **RSI makes higher low** → Bullish divergence

This tells you that momentum is fading despite price continuing to move — a warning that the trend is exhausting.

## Types of Divergence

### Regular Bullish Divergence
- Price: Lower low
- RSI: Higher low
- **Signal**: Downtrend losing momentum → potential reversal up
- **Reliability**: 60-65% at [support levels](/blog/crypto-support-resistance-trading)

### Regular Bearish Divergence
- Price: Higher high
- RSI: Lower high
- **Signal**: Uptrend losing momentum → potential reversal down
- **Reliability**: 58-63% at resistance levels

### Hidden Bullish Divergence (Continuation)
- Price: Higher low
- RSI: Lower low
- **Signal**: Pullback in uptrend is temporary → trend continues up
- Used for re-entry in existing trends

### Hidden Bearish Divergence (Continuation)
- Price: Lower high
- RSI: Higher high
- **Signal**: Bounce in downtrend is temporary → trend continues down

## How to Trade RSI Divergence

### Step 1: Identify the Divergence
Use RSI(14) on your trading timeframe. Look for clear divergence at extremes:
- Bullish divergence works best when RSI is below 40
- Bearish divergence works best when RSI is above 60

### Step 2: Wait for Confirmation
Divergence alone is not enough. Wait for:
- [Candlestick pattern](/blog/crypto-candlestick-patterns-guide) (engulfing, hammer, shooting star)
- Break of short-term trendline
- [Volume spike](/blog/crypto-volume-analysis-guide) in reversal direction

### Step 3: Enter with Tight Risk
```
Bullish divergence at support:
Entry: Above confirmation candle
Stop: Below the swing low (divergence low)
Target: Previous swing high or 2:1 R:R

Bearish divergence at resistance:
Entry: Below confirmation candle
Stop: Above the swing high (divergence high)
Target: Previous swing low or 2:1 R:R
```

## Common Divergence Mistakes

1. **Trading divergence in strong trends**: In powerful trends, RSI can show bearish divergence for weeks while price continues higher. Always confirm with a reversal signal.
2. **Wrong timeframe**: Divergence on 1-minute charts is unreliable. Daily and 4H are most effective.
3. **Ignoring context**: Bullish divergence in a massive downtrend with no support nearby is a losing trade.
4. **No confirmation**: Entering on divergence alone without a confirming candle or break.

## FAQ

### Is RSI divergence the best indicator signal?
For reversals, yes — RSI divergence combined with a key S/R level is among the highest-probability setups in all of technical analysis (60-65% accuracy). For trend-following, [moving average crossovers](/blog/crypto-moving-averages-strategy) are better.

### What RSI settings should I use for divergence?
RSI(14) is the standard and most tested. Some traders use RSI(9) for more signals (but more noise) or RSI(21) for fewer, higher-quality signals. Stick with 14 until you have a specific reason to change.

### Can divergence last a long time before reversing?
Yes — divergence can persist for days to weeks in strong trends. This is why confirmation is essential. Divergence tells you momentum is fading, but it doesn't tell you exactly when the reversal happens. Wait for the confirming candle or break.

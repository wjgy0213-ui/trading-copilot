## Detailed Breakdown

### Binance
**The default choice for most traders worldwide.**

**Pros:**
- Deepest liquidity globally
- Lowest spot fees with BNB discount (0.075%)
- Most comprehensive product suite
- Best mobile app
- Binance Earn for yield

**Cons:**
- Regulatory uncertainty in some countries
- US users restricted (use Binance.US with fewer features)
- Account freezes reported during extreme volatility
- Complex interface for beginners

**Fee structure:**
- Spot: 0.10% (0.075% with BNB)
- Futures: 0.02% maker / 0.05% taker
- Withdrawal fees vary by network

### Coinbase

**Pros:**
- Publicly traded (NASDAQ: COIN) — highest transparency
- Best regulatory compliance
- FDIC insurance on USD deposits
- Coinbase Advanced (formerly Pro) has competitive fees
- Best fiat on/off ramp

**Cons:**
- Significantly higher fees on basic platform
- Limited futures access in US
- Customer support notoriously slow
- Fewer altcoins than competitors

### Bybit

## Security Considerations

### What to Look For
1. **Proof of Reserves**: Does the exchange regularly prove they hold all customer funds?
2. **Insurance fund**: Coverage for security breaches
3. **2FA options**: Hardware key support (not just SMS)
4. **Withdrawal whitelisting**: Lock withdrawals to approved addresses
5. **Cold storage ratio**: What percentage of funds are in cold storage?
6. **Track record**: Has the exchange ever been hacked?

## Pro Tips for Exchange Usage

1. **Use maker orders**: Limit orders get lower fees than market orders on every exchange
2. **Hold exchange tokens**: BNB (Binance), OKB (OKX) for fee discounts
3. **Multi-exchange setup**: Keep different amounts for different strategies
4. **Don't store long-term holds on exchanges**: Use hardware wallets for anything you're not actively trading
5. **Check withdrawal networks**: Withdrawing via L2 (Arbitrum, Optimism) is much cheaper than L1
6. **Track your trading costs**: Use a [trading journal](/blog/crypto-trading-journal-guide) to monitor fee impact on your P&L

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest crypto exchange for trading?
For spot trading, Binance with BNB fee discount (0.075%) is the cheapest major exchange. For perpetual futures, Hyperliquid offers the lowest fees at 0.01% maker / 0.035% taker. Always factor in withdrawal fees when comparing total costs.

### Is Coinbase safe for large amounts?
Coinbase is one of the safest exchanges — it's publicly traded, FDIC-insured for USD deposits, and has never been hacked. However, for long-term storage of large amounts, a hardware wallet is always safer than any exchange.

### Should I use multiple crypto exchanges?
Yes, using 2-3 exchanges is recommended. Keep your main trading account on one exchange for lower fees (volume tiers), but maintain accounts on others for coin availability, backup access, and diversification of counterparty risk.

### What happens if a crypto exchange gets hacked?
It depends on the exchange. Binance covered its 2019 hack from its SAFU insurance fund. Coinbase has insurance for covered assets. Smaller exchanges may not be able to make users whole. This is why exchange selection and self-custody matter.

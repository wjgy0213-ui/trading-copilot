## Detailed Exchange Reviews

### Binance — The Everything Exchange
**Best for**: Most traders (unless US-based)

**Strengths**:
- Deepest liquidity in crypto
- Lowest maker fees at scale (0.02%)
- Most comprehensive feature set (spot, futures, options, earn, launchpad)
- BNB discount reduces fees by 25%

**Weaknesses**:
- Regulatory uncertainty in some jurisdictions
- Complex UI for beginners
- Customer support can be slow

### Coinbase — The US Standard
**Best for**: US-based beginners and investors

**Strengths**:
- Publicly traded (NASDAQ: COIN) = maximum transparency
- Best regulatory compliance in crypto
- Simple interface (Coinbase) + pro interface (Advanced Trade)
- BTC ETF custodian (institutional trust)

**Weaknesses**:
- Highest fees among major exchanges
- Limited futures/leverage for US users
- No margin trading for retail

### Bybit — The Derivatives King
**Best for**: Futures and perpetual traders

**Strengths**:
- Excellent derivatives platform
- Copy trading feature
- Competitive fee structure
- Good mobile app

**Weaknesses**:
- Less trusted than Binance/Coinbase
- Not available in all jurisdictions
- Spot selection smaller than Binance

### Hyperliquid — The DeFi Future
**Best for**: Cost-conscious futures traders

**Strengths**:
- Lowest fees in the industry (0.01% maker)
- On-chain transparency (all trades verifiable)
- No KYC required
- Fast execution

**Weaknesses**:
- Smaller liquidity than CEXs
- Limited coin selection
- Newer platform (less battle-tested)
- Smart contract risk

## Security Considerations

### Exchange Security Checklist
- [ ] Proof of reserves published and audited?
- [ ] Cold storage majority (95%+)?
- [ ] Insurance fund for user losses?
- [ ] Two-factor authentication (hardware key support)?
- [ ] Withdrawal whitelist with cooling period?
- [ ] Never been hacked? (If hacked, how was it handled?)

### Your Security Checklist
- [ ] Enable 2FA (Authenticator app, NOT SMS)
- [ ] Use a unique password (password manager)
- [ ] Set withdrawal whitelist addresses
- [ ] Enable anti-phishing code
- [ ] Regularly review API key permissions
- [ ] Don't keep more than you need on exchange

## FAQ

### Which crypto exchange is safest?
Coinbase and Kraken have the strongest security track records. Coinbase is publicly traded and serves as the custodian for BlackRock's IBIT Bitcoin ETF. Kraken has never been hacked and maintains excellent security practices. For maximum safety, keep only trading capital on exchange and self-custody the rest.

### Is Binance safe to use?
Binance is safe from a technical security standpoint and publishes proof of reserves. However, it faces ongoing regulatory scrutiny in multiple jurisdictions and paid a $4.3B fine in 2023. For trading purposes, it remains the most liquid exchange. For long-term storage, consider withdrawing to self-custody.

### What's the cheapest crypto exchange for day trading?
For futures: Hyperliquid (0.01% maker) or Binance with BNB discount (0.015% maker). For spot: Binance (0.075% with BNB) or OKX. The key is reaching higher fee tiers through volume and using limit orders for maker rates.

### Should I use one exchange or multiple?
Use 2-3 exchanges: one primary for trading (best fees/features for your style), one backup (in case of downtime/restrictions), and optionally one for specialized needs (like DeFi or copy trading). Avoid spreading too thin — it makes tracking harder.

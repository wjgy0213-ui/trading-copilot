## The Core Difference

**Trading Copilot** is built for people who want to **become better traders.** It focuses on practice, coaching, and understanding.

**3Commas and Cryptohopper** are built for people who already know how to trade and want to **automate their strategies.**

If you're asking "should I go long or short on BTC?" — you need Trading Copilot.
If you're asking "how do I run my proven strategy 24/7 while I sleep?" — you need 3Commas or Cryptohopper.

## Detailed Breakdown

### For Beginners (0-6 months experience)

**Winner: Trading Copilot** ✅

Why: Beginners don't need automated bots. They need to understand the market, develop strategies, and build discipline. Giving a beginner a trading bot is like giving a student driver a race car — it won't end well.

Trading Copilot provides:
- Risk-free paper trading with live market data
- AI coach that scores every trade and explains what you did right/wrong
- Emotional pattern detection (catches FOMO, revenge trading)
- Structured learning path from basics to advanced

### For Intermediate Traders (6-24 months)

**Winner: Tie** — Trading Copilot for practice, 3Commas for automation

At this stage, you might use both:
- **Trading Copilot** for backtesting new strategies and Monte Carlo analysis
- **3Commas** for automating your proven strategies on exchange

### For Advanced Traders (2+ years)

**Winner: 3Commas or Cryptohopper**

Advanced traders have proven strategies and need execution tools, not learning tools. However, even experienced traders benefit from Trading Copilot's [Market Health](https://tradingcopilot.app/health) and [Signal Aggregator](https://tradingcopilot.app/signals) as decision-support layers.

## FAQ

### Can I use Trading Copilot with 3Commas?

They serve different purposes and don't directly integrate. But many traders use Trading Copilot for analysis and learning, then execute trades through 3Commas bots.

### Is it better to trade manually or use bots?

Start manually. You need to understand what your strategy does before automating it. A bot running a bad strategy just loses money faster.

### Which has the best free tier?

Trading Copilot — 3 free analysis per day, full paper trading, and basic courses. 3Commas free is very limited. Cryptohopper has no permanent free tier.
